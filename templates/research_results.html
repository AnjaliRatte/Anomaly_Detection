Experimental Benchmark Summary

Comprehensive performance evaluation and visual analytics across all six anomaly detection models on the NAB dataset.

Top Balanced Performer
Isolation Forest leads with the highest PR-AUC (0.365) and F1-score (0.106) while maintaining a fast execution time of 1.48s.
High Recall Trade-off
DBSCAN achieves the highest recall (0.808), but suffers from lower precision and high false alarm rates.
Deep Learning Cost
LSTM Autoencoder captures temporal dynamics effectively, but requires heavy compute time (~820s).
